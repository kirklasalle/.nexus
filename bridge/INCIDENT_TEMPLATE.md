# Nexus Bridge Incident Template

Use this template when a bridge issue is critical, cross-cutting, or requires a durable timeline.

```markdown
# Incident: [Short Title]

---
**Incident ID:** INC-YYYY-MM-DD-XX
**Date Detected:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM EST
**Owner:** [Name]
**Severity:** [Critical | High | Medium | Low]
**Status:** [Investigating | Mitigating | Resolved | Post-Mortem]
**Affected Areas:** [Bridge files, agents, workflows]
**Sensitivity:** [Internal | Confidential | Restricted]
---

## Summary
[One-paragraph description of the incident.]

## Timeline
- HH:MM - Issue detected
- HH:MM - Escalated
- HH:MM - Root cause identified
- HH:MM - Mitigation applied
- HH:MM - Verification complete

## Root Cause
[What failed and why.]

## Resolution
[What changed to restore normal operation.]

## Follow-Up Actions
- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]
```
